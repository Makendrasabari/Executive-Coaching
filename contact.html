Common Questions

Executive Coaching — Frequently Asked Questions

Our coaching engagements typically run 6 or 12 months, with bi-weekly one-on-one sessions. The duration is determined by the scope of your leadership goals, the specific program selected, and your organizational context. Shorter intensive engagements of 3 months are available for targeted transitions or specific performance challenges.
Coach-executive matching is one of the most important aspects of a successful engagement. We consider your industry background, leadership challenges, personality profile, and coaching objectives alongside each coach's specific expertise, style, and sector experience. We always facilitate an introduction meeting before formalizing the relationship, and if the match isn't right, we will rematch at no additional cost.
Absolute confidentiality is the foundation of every Stackly engagement. All coaching conversations, assessments, personal disclosures, and organizational information shared during coaching are strictly confidential. We operate under a formal confidentiality protocol and will never share client information without explicit written consent.
Yes. The majority of our engagements are funded by the executive's organisation as a strategic leadership investment. We work directly with CHROs, Talent teams, and CFOs to structure commercially viable arrangements. We can also accommodate individually funded engagements for executives who prefer complete independence from their organisation.
Executive coaching is a structured, evidence-based professional relationship focused on developing your specific leadership capabilities and achieving measurable outcomes. Unlike mentoring, coaching does not rely on the coach's personal experience as the primary source of guidance. Unlike consulting, it does not deliver external solutions — it develops internal capacity. Our coaches combine the rigour of professional coaching with deep executive experience to offer perspectives that draw on all three disciplines where appropriate.
We offer both modalities. The majority of our coaching is delivered virtually via secure video conferencing — providing flexibility for senior executives with demanding travel schedules. We also offer in-person sessions at our London and New York offices, or at a location of your preference. Many executives choose a hybrid approach combining virtual sessions with periodic in-person intensive sessions.
